# Frameworks

This section captures how CryptoWisely approaches complex crypto, Web3, and digital asset problems **before execution begins**.

Our frameworks are not templates or playbooks.  
They are **decision architectures** designed to reduce regulatory, structural, and market-access risk upstream.

We focus on:
- sequencing decisions correctly,
- separating representation, ownership, custody, and settlement,
- and preserving optionality under regulatory constraints.

Each framework reflects real advisory work, shared at an architecture level to illustrate **how we think**, not what we sell.
