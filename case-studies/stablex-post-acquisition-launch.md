# Case 3 – Stablex (Akbank Group): Post-Acquisition Brand Launch & Trust-Building

**Position:** Marketing Director  
**Period:** 2022 – 2023  
**Scope:** Brand awareness, community engagement, PR activation, and organic growth

---

## Objectives

1. Establish Stablex as **Akbank’s official crypto subsidiary** (trust, security, credibility).  
2. Build strong awareness among **institutional investors** and **high-income retail** users.  
3. Achieve **cost-efficient growth** using an organic, PR-first strategy.  
4. Reach **1 million users in 6 months** while keeping acquisition cost and revenue in balance.

---

## Approach & Implementation

### Brand Positioning & Messaging

- Introduced the message **“Stablex – Invest with Confidence”**.  
- Redesigned identity to combine bank-level reliability with crypto innovation.  
- Emphasized **“An Akbank Group Company”** on all channels to strengthen credibility.

### PR & Media Strategy (Organic-First)

- Prioritized **earned media** versus heavy paid advertising.  
- Interviews and thought-leadership in **Bloomberg HT, Webrazzi, Capital, Para Dergisi**.  
- Hosted press briefings and financial panels with Akbank & Stablex executives.  
- Built an organic trust narrative via editorials and storytelling.

### Digital & Social Media Engagement

- Used **LinkedIn** as the primary reputation and B2B channel.  
- Distributed educational content on **Twitter (X), Instagram, YouTube**.  
- Limited influencer spend; focused on organic collaborations and community reposts.  
- Managed **Telegram & Discord** for support, education, and engagement.

### Events & Community Relations

- Participated in **Akbank Fintech Days** and key industry conferences.  
- Ran “Secure Investment Workshops” with universities and fintech hubs.  
- Organized closed briefings for SMEs and corporate clients exploring regulated crypto.

---

## Budget & Outcome Summary

- **Budget split:** 40% digital • 30% PR & content • 20% events • 10% community  
- Within **6 months**, Stablex:

  - Surpassed **1M users**  
  - Maintained **low CAC** through PR-first strategy  
  - Achieved a **positive cost–revenue balance**

Stablex emerged as **Türkiye’s first trust-led institutional crypto platform** backed by a major bank.

## Key Takeaways

This phase reflected a **trust-led growth** model:

- credibility over hype  
- education over speculation  
- community over one-off campaigns  

By combining **Akbank’s reputation** with organic PR and digital strategy, Stablex reached sustainable scale while optimizing acquisition costs.
