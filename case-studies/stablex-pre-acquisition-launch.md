# Case 2 – Stablex (Ak Yatırım / Akbank Group): Pre-Acquisition Launch & Investor Communication

**Position:** Marketing Director  
**Period:** 2021 – 2022  
**Scope:** Brand positioning, investor relations, and strategic launch planning

---

## Objectives

1. Build the **Stablex** brand identity and positioning before the Akbank acquisition.  
2. Strengthen **investor trust** and transparency with clear communication and forecasting.  
3. Prepare the brand for a **regulated, institutionally credible** launch in Türkiye’s financial ecosystem.

---

## Approach & Implementation

### 1. Pre-Launch Brand Development

- Developed the concept **“Stablex – Secure Crypto Experience”** (tone, visuals, messaging).  
- Built an integrated media plan (TV, YouTube, LinkedIn, PR) targeting retail + institutional audiences.  
- Ran a **4-week pre-launch awareness** phase focused on HNWI and institutional partners.

### 2. Investor Communication & Roadshow Series

- Created investor decks and whitepaper drafts with ROI projections and product roadmap.  
- Led **roadshows across 8 countries**, meeting fintech VCs, institutional investors, and regulators.  
- Positioned **Akbank’s financial credibility** as the backbone of Stablex messaging (trust, transparency, compliance).  
- Managed investor communication via LinkedIn, newsletters, and 1:1 updates with weekly progress visuals.

### 3. Launch Preparation & Media Synchronization

- Defined public message **“Stable & Smart Investing”** — merging security with innovation.  
- Produced educational PR content explaining regulatory alignment and security model.  
- Aligned social, PR, and media interviews into a single unified brand narrative.

---

## Highlights

- Built strong **investor trust** and brand equity prior to acquisition.  
- Positioned Stablex as a **compliance-ready, institutional-grade** crypto platform.  
- Recognized by **Akbank leadership** as a benchmark fintech launch model.

---

| Institutional Partnerships | 5        | 12           | +140 %               |

---

## Key Takeaways

This campaign showed how **corporate trust + investor transparency** can accelerate entry into a regulated fintech market.  

A solid pre-launch roadmap, cross-border investor communication, and regulatory awareness positioned Stablex for:

- a successful acquisition  
- long-term credibility under **Akbank Group**.
